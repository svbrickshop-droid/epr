EU Packaging EPR Map

What a small online seller from another EU country runs into when shipping parcels to consumers in each member state: the packaging scheme, whether there is a minimum quantity, and what it costs to stay compliant.

Last checked11 Nov 202627 member states

Since 12 August 2026 the PPWR applies in every EU country, and it has no EU-wide minimum quantity for registering. Countries are still adapting their national rules, so much of this is moving. This page is information, not legal advice: confirm the details with the scheme before you ship.

How to read this

No small-seller threshold: obligations start with your first parcel. Lighter rules below a threshold: below a set quantity you still have duties, but fewer. Exempt below a threshold: below the quantity you do not register or pay. Not published yet: no official threshold found; some guides mention one, but we could not confirm it.

Fixed costs and reporting are left as "not published" where we found no reliable figure. Most fees are charged per kilo of packaging, which is cents for a small seller; the fixed yearly fees and paperwork are what weigh on small shops.
